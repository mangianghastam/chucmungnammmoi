Cheers to a new year, so bright and clear,
A year filled with joy, no sorrow or fear.
May your heart be light, your dreams come true,
With every step, may happiness pursue.

May good fortune knock at your door,
And bring blessings you’ll adore.
A year of laughter, love, and cheer,
Wishing you a wonderful New Year!
